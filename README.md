## About

I write backend code and build AI applications. Most of my day-to-day work is in **TypeScript, Node.js, and Python**, with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis behind it.

At **Mantri AI**, I worked on CV screening, search across **15,000+ resumes**, and recruiter workflows connected to Zoho. Before that, I worked on platform design and development at **GeeksforGeeks** and mentored students at **Apna College**.

The projects below cover a few problems I've worked on: sorting email, checking a story for contradictions, and helping students find jobs. They also involve the backend work I like getting into: queues, caching, retrieval, and retry handling.

I'm studying Computer Science and Engineering (AI) at **RGIPT** and expect to graduate in **May 2027**.

## Experience

**Mantri AI · Full Stack AI Engineer Intern**  
May–October 2026 · Bengaluru

- Built a screening agent that parses CVs and scores them against job descriptions. An analyst–judge revision loop reviews the output before approved results are written to Zoho Recruit, with retries and restart-safe processing.
- Worked on search across **15,000+ CVs**, combining hard filters, pgvector retrieval, and parallel LLM reranking. The ingestion pipeline included **73K PII-redacted embeddings**, evidence verification, recruiter approval, and audit logs.
- Connected scheduling and interview workflows to Google Calendar, Zoho, email, and Slack. Model benchmarking and OpenAI Batch API experiments reduced **projected AI processing costs by 82%**.
